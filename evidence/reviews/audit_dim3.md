# Three-dimensional manuscript audit (independent exploratory review)

Date: 2026-10-07. This is a source-assisted mathematical audit, not a Danus accepted fact and not a proof-assistant certificate. Scope: the two short three-dimensional proofs and a complete numbered dependency inventory of the 6192-line long manuscript, with detailed checks of selected mechanisms; the inventory does not certify every statement. ## Current conclusion

No fatal error has been found in either three-dimensional short proof in the audited steps. The uncompleted geometric proof has a coherent independent chain; the completed explicit proof has a coherent chain conditional on its reconstructed AMMN module-compatible relative character, for which the cited source constructions support the asserted refinement. This report does not certify every extension in the 86-page long manuscript: the higher-rank, all-prime, all-degree, syntomic normalization, and infinite-family sections have distinct dependencies and need their own acceptance records.

## Exact objects and generic vanishing

* `gersten_explicit_short_proof.tex:80–138`: A=(Z_(3)[x,y,z]/(3+xy+z^2))_(x,y,z), completion Z_3[[x,y,z]]/(3+xy+z^2). Ambient dimension four; relation has nonzero p-linear class, quotient dimension three and maximal ideal generated by x,y,z. Thus both are regular local domains and p lies in m^2.
* All entries of c=2{(1+z)/2,1+x,1+(1+x)y/4} and 1−a are units. The arithmetic identity a(1−a)=1+xy/4 is exact, using 3+xy+z^2=0.
* Dennis–Stein convention is consistent: <r,s>={r,1−rs} when r is a unit, and <r,s>={1−rs,s} when s is a unit. With r=−x,t=y/4, adding <−x,1>=0 replaces t with t+1+xt=mu; 1+x mu=lambda q. The extra q product is annihilated by 2 because {a,1−a}=0 and 2{a,a}=0 in Quillen K. Thus c=2[a]delta and vanishes integrally after inverting x. This uses no conjectural Gersten statement.

## Independent geometric route

`geometric_short_counterexample.tex:80–425`:

1. R_D=A/(x)=W[y]_(u,y) is regular, W=Z_(p)[u]/(u^2+p) is a DVR, E=Q(u) imaginary quadratic for odd p. Localization K3(W)→K3(E) is onto since K2(F_p)=0. Antisymmetrization beta=b−sigma b gives sigma beta=−beta and a nonzero point regulator. c=i_*beta is an actual class with support x=0, hence generic image zero.
2. B=A⊗W is local because its finite integral closed fiber has one maximal ideal, so B=(B0)_n. Its fraction field extension remains a field because Q is algebraically closed in Q(x,z); the irreducible quadratic stays irreducible. B is a normal three-dimensional node; the usual Jacobian/local hypersurface argument makes it regular off its closed point.
3. Blowup of I=(x,z−u) has actual saturated Rees charts W[x,t] and W[q,y], localized at original denominators. The exceptional fiber is P1_Fp, dimension one, hence codimension two; no exceptional divisor exists. The tautological ideal restricts as O(1) on this curve. Its height-one divisor is P_+, so O(tilde P_+)|F=O(−1). Any equation div(f)=N P_+ would force O(−N) trivial on F, hence N=0. The chart saturation is essential and is included in the manuscript.
4. B/(u) is the integral quadric cone; inverting u removes only the principal prime (u). Divisor-class localization therefore gives Cl(B)≅Pic(B[1/u]), preserving the infinite-order ruling.
5. Q is (P1×P1) minus a (1,1) divisor. Its geometric Picard group is Z, generated by the E-defined ruling, and its complex H2 is Z with that first Chern class. Pic(Q)→Pic(T) is injective.
6. Crucial finite-localization step at lines 250–305 is stronger than merely observing that div(s) is principal. Each E-prime boundary divisor is itself absent from T and hence has zero class on T; Pic injectivity makes its class zero on Q. Its geometric irreducible components form one Galois orbit; Galois acts trivially on the torsion-free rank-one Picard group, so each individual component has class zero. The support-cohomology argument then makes H2(Q_C,R)→H2((Q_s)_C,R) injective. Deleting finitely many singular/intersection points is legitimate because local cohomology of a smooth complex surface supported at points begins in degree four.
7. For a complex surface, analytic Deligne weight three is H2(C)/H2(R(3)), since F3 of the holomorphic de Rham complex is zero. An injective map of real H2 groups induces an injective map of these quotients. The product of beta's it coefficient (mod R(2)) with 2pi i h gives −2pi t h, nonzero modulo the imaginary line R(3)h.
8. The K-theory class beta eta has nonzero regulator at EVERY finite Q_s. Continuity then proves infinite order on T; no regulator on the infinite localization T is required. Base change c|T=beta([OD+]−[OD−])=2 beta eta is valid because the branches are disjoint after inverting u and their sum is the principal divisor of x. Hence c has infinite order already over A.

Published reference checked: Bunke–Tamme, https://msp.org/akt/2016/1-3/akt-v1-n3-p01-s.pdf, Remark 2.6 (analytic Deligne), Remark 2.30 (scope distinction), Theorem 2.31 (ring-spectrum regulator), §6 equation (6.4) (Borel comparison). Their Reg_Z scope is handled by spreading each finite collection to O_E[1/N], which preserves the complex fiber. These citations actually support the asserted scope; they are not references to a result claiming the new counterexample.

## Completed explicit route

`gersten_explicit_short_proof.tex:140–420`:

* AMMN https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.12541, Proposition 4.10, p.22, really supplies both squares for a smooth formal O_K-scheme, with no properness hypothesis. It applies to B=W<X,Y,Z>/(XY+Z^2−1), W=Z3[u]/(u^2+3).
* Its horizontal bottom fiber is ΣHC, so actual relative K3 maps to HC2 and the top component is Ω2/dΩ1. The ordinary quotient, rather than quotient by the closure, is important and legitimate. Proposition 4.2 identifies the p-completed continuous invariants. In relative dimension two and p=3, normalized HKR 1/n! is integral through the only nonzero Hochschild degrees n≤2, giving the finite mixed complex. No infinite denominator issue occurs here.
* The manuscript reconstructs K(B)-linearity from actual THH(B)-module maps in AMMN Proposition 2.8 / Corollary 2.10 / Theorem 2.12. Reading these original constructions supports the claim: the tensor factors and arrows id_H⊗f, the fixed-point/Tate maps, and the relative Hochschild projection carry the induced action. Inversion of equivalences can be done in the module category. This is a reconstructed refinement, not a verbatim statement of Proposition 4.10; it is the principal interface to submit to a dedicated verifier.
* Relative unit normalization is determined on W<T>: its image f in HC0 is characterized by df=dlog(1+uT) and f(0)=0, giving f=log(1+uT). This uses only the canonical absolute trace map to Hochschild homology and the chosen norm sign. Naturality then gives the general relative logarithm.
* The finite mixed-complex module calculation gives log(v) dlog(b1) wedge dlog(b2). Absolute trace components beyond the column-zero unit term and the cyclic-shuffle correction are killed by the positive cyclic column quotient when acting on this representative. A global sign cannot affect nonvanishing.
* Actual-to-relative issue is explicitly resolved: special fiber C is an A1-torsor over P1_F3, hence K4(C)=0 for ACTUAL groups by homotopy invariance and the projective bundle formula. Thus K3(B,uB)→K3(B) is injective. Nonzero rational character of the specified actual relative lift proves infinite order of the actual absolute element, rather than merely nonzero spectral completion.
* The map from the completed local ring is legitimate: x,y,z↦uX,uY,uZ is topologically nilpotent and every original formal series converges. It preserves the relation.

### Residue lemma

Lines 293–328: deleting the other X=0 root gives two formal affine bidiscs, with intersection W<X,X^−1,Z>. The primitives q_i=−(F(Z)−F(a_i))dX/X are regular because Z−a_i=Xt_i. For a closed form A dX+B dt, substitution t=c/X gives residue sum_m(a_(m,m+1)−b_(m+1,m))c^(m+1), whose coefficients vanish by closedness. The sum converges because coefficients of a restricted power series tend to zero. Dividing each equation by m+1 in the coefficient field does not assert the existence of a bounded primitive. Taking Z=0 on the overlap is legal also if one c=0. Therefore exactness implies F(a0)=F(a1).

### Endpoint calculation

Lines 370–414: the form identity follows from q=1+xt, lambda=1+x, mu=1+lambda t. Direct algebra gives dlog(lambda) wedge dlog(mu)=dlog(x) wedge dlog(q)−dlog(q) wedge dlog(mu). With dF=L dlog q, tau is 2(dX/X) wedge dF modulo an exact global form.

The odd part of L is u sum_k (-3)^k Z^(2k+1)/(2k+1). Multiplying by 6Z/(1+3Z^2) and integrating from −1 to 1 yields exactly (F(1)−F(−1))/u=12 sum_j (-3)^j S_j/(2j+3). Initial terms are 4, −48/5, 828/35, −2112/35, 60804/385. The j≥1 tail has valuation at least one and tends to +infinity. The floor bound used for j≥4 follows from bases j=4,5 and the two-step inequality 2j+7<3(2j+3). Hence normalized value is 4 mod 3, in particular nonzero.

An independent exact-rational Python check computed the first 100 terms, found each j≥1 term divisible by 3, and sum mod3=1; it also checked the floor inequality up to j=9999. These are sanity checks only. The infinite tail is covered by the written valuation/induction argument, not by finite testing.

### S1-HKR 与 essentially smooth 延拓的具体审计

13.6 的关键不只是在 rational HH 上使用 HKR。以 q_j=(D!/j!) a0 da1∧…∧daj 构造 integral mixed map；Connes B 的 j+1项补偿归一化，故 qB=dq。ordinary HKR 后每个同调映射是 D!。由于 form modules W-flat，cone 只有0…D阶的 cokernel Ω^j/D!，没有顶端额外kernel。Functor category Fun(BS1,D(W)) 的逐点 t-structure 给有限 Postnikov tower；heart module的 Aut space 离散、BS1 simply connected，故 action trivial。p^{v_p(D!)}杀每层；三角的extension argument给 p^{(D+1)v_p(D!)}杀整个 equivariant cone。因此 fixed points/Tate/derived completion 之后误差仍受同一幂控制，而不是错误地交换 rationalization 与无穷乘积。这段与 `work/hkr_repair.tex` 的独立补证一致，适用于长稿的 p=3、D=3 与任意固定D；不能沿用该补证末段专属 p=5 时 D!可逆的说法。

有限 form mixed complex在任何总degree只有有限项。其π-adic reduction tower逐项满射，故 derived completion 是普通termwise completion；所得 de Rham商是普通image商，无需闭包。unit multiplication 的正cyclic columns消失依赖13.1的module identification，应与上面的HKR有限误差分别门控。

13.26 的新输入是 W0=W(Fbarp)、W/W0全分歧且 B0允许无限 localization。核查如下：

1. W0/p^r 是 finite etale Z/p^r-algebras 的filtered union，因此 completed cotangent为零；Eisenstein W给二项式 [W --f′(π)→ W]，被固定p^a作为对象杀死。
2. relative Ω¹=P 是B上finite projective degree0。M→Lhat→P 的connecting map P→M[1]零，故underlying B-complex拆分；不能把此拆分误说成canonical或equivariant。文稿只用underlying splitting来估 exterior errors，范围合适。
3. derived Λ^r(p^a id_M)=p^{ar} id，因此每个固定r的exterior error有有界幂。HKR graded pieces随i越来越connective；derived p-completion保持connectivity（底阶π/p^r的lim1因满射为零），所以每个固定degree只需有限多层。此论证给 absolute→relative HH 完成后[1/p] equivalence，不提供一个统一于所有degree的幂；文稿并未使用后者。
4. 从HH到HC（orbits）时每个固定degree只需有限circle skeleton；可把文稿此步进一步明写为：先对误差的有限Postnikov截断应用上述equivariant有限extension bound，再取足够高skeleton。这样明确补上从“各底层同调有限范围有界”到“等变orbits有限范围有界”的接口。只需HC等价即可由norm fiber sequence得HC−→HP square cartesian，不需宣称两条vertical individually等价。
5. complete B沿p henselian，AMMN Theorem A适用任意这样的ring；B/p→B/π nilpotent kernel由其Cor3.8处理。module refinement与13.1一样是重建输入；AMMN4.2原文已核对：任意qcqs且bounded p-torsion即可；4.10前一段明确relative variants亦适用，故此处无需finite presentation。

结论：13.26内部延拓逻辑已逐接口阅读并补足第4点的可写明论证，暂未找到反例性错误；鉴于涉及绝对derived HKR filtration、p-completion和equivariant action的组合，它仍是 C 状态，不能只因 AMMN4.10存在便声称独立验证完成。
