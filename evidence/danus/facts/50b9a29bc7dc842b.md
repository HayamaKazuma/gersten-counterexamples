## statement
Bounded elliptic contraction and a split injection after generic Cohen completion. Let k be an algebraic closure of F_5, W=W(k), K=W[1/5], E_0/k the elliptic curve v^2=z^3+z with origin O, and E_0^circ=E_0\{O,(0,0)}. Let A be the coordinate ring of the same affine curve lifted over W and Ahat its 5-adic completion. Choose rational dagger one-forms omega and eta on A representing respectively nonzero generators of the slope-one and slope-zero lines in proper crystalline H^1(E_0/W)[1/5], restricted to E_0^circ. Let D be any smooth affine W-algebra of relative dimension one with connected nonempty special fiber, let C be the 5-adic completion of its localization at the generic special point, and set B=(Ahat⊗_W C)^wedge_5. All forms on completed rings below are continuous, and all cohomology quotients use ordinary images of differentials. There is a bounded K-linear functional ell:Omega^1_(Ahat/W)[1/5]→K with ell(omega)=1, ell(eta)=0, and ell(dAhat[1/5])=0. It induces a continuous degree-minus-one chain operator L:Omega^bullet_(B/W)[1/5]→Omega^bullet_(C/W)[1/5][-1] satisfying L_2(alpha∧gamma)=ell(alpha)gamma for an elliptic one-form alpha and a base one-form gamma. The homomorphism J:H^1_dR(C/W)[1/5]→H^2_dR(B/W)[1/5], [gamma]↦[omega∧gamma], is split injective with left inverse induced by L. In particular a two-form whose class has a representative omega∧gamma+eta∧delta is nonzero whenever [gamma] is nonzero in Omega^1_(C/W)[1/5]/d(C[1/5]).

## proof
1. Published slope input and applicability. The cubic z^3+z has distinct roots in characteristic 5 and the projective Weierstrass curve is smooth. The coefficient of z^4 in (z^3+z)^2 is 2, nonzero modulo 5, so the Hasse invariant criterion gives ordinarity. Thus its proper rational crystalline H^1 has two one-dimensional slope spaces, of slopes zero and one. Restriction to the complement of two points injects proper H^1 into rigid H^1: the localization (Gysin) sequence has preceding term H^(-1) of the removed zero-dimensional scheme, which vanishes. The restriction respects Frobenius.

We use Daniel Caro and Marco D'Addezio, Injectivity failure in crystalline comparisons, arXiv:2511.11444. In its notation N_conv^r denotes the closure of zero in the affinoid topology of convergent cohomology. The needed statements are: Theorem 7.3.1 says that for a smooth scheme over a perfect field with unramified Witt coefficient ring, the slope-r comparison H^r_rig[r]→H^r_conv[r] is bijective. Theorem 7.3.6 says that for a smooth affine scheme and unit-root coefficient isocrystal, H^r_conv[r] intersects N_conv^r trivially. Theorem 7.2.1 says that for a smooth affine scheme and pure-slope-gamma coefficient, its cohomology of slopes in [gamma,gamma+r) is contained in N_conv^r for r>=1. Apply these with the smooth affine curve E_0^circ, constant coefficient of slope zero, and r=1. The coefficient ring is W and is unramified. These give precisely that the image of omega is nonzero in separated convergent cohomology, whereas the image of eta is in the closure of zero.

For this affine smooth lift let M_0=Omega^1_(Ahat/W) and M=M_0[1/5]. Since the relative dimension is one, all one-forms are closed. Definition 7.1.1 of that paper identifies convergent H^1 with M/d(Ahat[1/5]), its affinoid topology with the topology defined by the lattices 5^nM_0, and its separated quotient with M/N, where N is the closure of d(Ahat[1/5]) in M. Thus omega has nonzero image in M/N and eta belongs to N. No separated quotient replaces any cohomology group in the conclusion; it is used here only to construct a bounded functional.

2. Construction of the functional. We include the functional-analytic step. The finite projective Ahat-module M_0 is complete, separated, and torsion-free, so its lattice norm makes M a Banach K-vector space with discrete norm values. The quotient V=M/N by the closed K-linear subspace N is again Banach for the quotient norm. The positive quotient norms belong to the same discrete value group: for a vector outside N, an infimum of the discrete norms of its representatives is positive and attained. Completeness of the quotient follows by lifting successive differences of a Cauchy subsequence with norms tending to zero and summing the lifts in M.

Write V_0 for the unit ball. Then V_0 is 5-adically complete and separated and its quotient V_0/5V_0 is a k-vector space. Choose a basis of this vector space and lift it to elements e_i of V_0. Every v in V_0 has a unique expansion v=sum_i a_i e_i, with a_i in W tending to zero off finite subsets of the index set. For existence, reduce v modulo 5, subtract the finite basis expansion with arbitrary integral lifts of its coefficients, divide the remainder by 5, and repeat. The resulting coefficient families are summable and the series converges in V_0. For uniqueness, a nonzero coefficient family tending to zero has a maximal absolute value; after scaling, reducing the finite set of maximal coefficients modulo 5 contradicts basis independence if the vector represented is zero. Scaling gives the same expansion with coefficients in K for every v in V. The coordinate maps v↦a_i are therefore K-linear and bounded. Since the image of omega in V is nonzero, one such coordinate map is nonzero on it. Divide that coordinate functional by its value at omega and compose M→V. The resulting ell is bounded, takes omega to 1, and annihilates N, hence annihilates both eta and all exact elliptic one-forms. Boundedness gives an integer e>=0 with 5^e ell(M_0)⊂W.

3. Extension to completed forms. Put ell_0=5^e ell. Reduce this integral W-linear map modulo 5^n, tensor it with C/5^nC, and take the inverse limit. This gives a continuous map M_0 completed-tensor C→C. Tensoring instead with Omega^1_(C/W)/5^n gives a map M_0 completed-tensor Omega^1_(C/W)→Omega^1_(C/W). Divide by 5^e after rationalization. Since these constructions take inverse limits of already compatible operators, no cohomology-limit interchange occurs.

At every finite precision, the algebra defining B is the tensor product of A/5^nA and C/5^nC over W/5^nW. The formula for differentials of a tensor product gives, on passage to the limit,
 Omega^1_(B/W)=(M_0 completed-tensor C) direct-sum (Ahat completed-tensor Omega^1_(C/W)),
 Omega^2_(B/W)=M_0 completed-tensor Omega^1_(C/W).
Forms in higher degrees vanish. Here both factors have continuous relative cotangent module of rank one; for C this follows by completing an etale-coordinate localization of the smooth relative curve D. These modules are finite projective over their respective rings. The mod-5^n tensor formulas and their limits are valid because the underlying W-modules are flat and their completed tensor products reduce to the original tensor products at finite precision.

Define L_0=0, define L_1 on the first summand by the extended ell and on the second summand by zero, and define L_2 by the second tensor extension. This is precisely the stated wedge formula, with the elliptic factor placed first.

4. Chain identity and splitting. It suffices to verify the identity on finite sums of pure tensors, which are dense; all operators, including relative derivations, are continuous. The elliptic derivative of a function is killed by ell, so L_1d_B=0 on functions. For an elliptic one-form alpha and a base function f,
 d_B(alpha f)=-alpha∧d_Cf
because d_Aalpha=0. Thus L_2d_B(alpha f)=-ell(alpha)d_Cf=-d_C L_1(alpha f).
For an elliptic function a and base one-form gamma,
 d_B(a gamma)=d_Aa∧gamma
because Omega^2_(C/W)=0. Applying L_2 gives ell(d_Aa)gamma=0, while L_1(a gamma)=0. Hence L_2d_B=-d_C L_1. This is the chain identity with the shift [-1].

For a base one-form gamma, omega∧gamma is closed. If gamma=d_Cf then omega∧gamma=-d_B(f omega), so J is well-defined on ordinary cohomology. Finally L_2(omega∧gamma)=gamma since ell(omega)=1. Therefore the induced maps satisfy L∘J=id and J is injective. The eta term is killed because ell(eta)=0. If a representative omega∧gamma+eta∧delta were exact, its image under the chain operator would make gamma exact in C[1/5], proving the last assertion.

## intuition
A bounded functional in the slope-one elliptic direction contracts the completed product onto the entire ordinary one-form quotient of the Cohen field; its annihilation of the unit-root elliptic direction isolates the base unit-root class.
