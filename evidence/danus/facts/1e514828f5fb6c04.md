## statement
Weighted quartic root Chern classes remain nonzero after Cohen completion. Let k be an algebraic closure of F_5, W=W(k), rho a primitive fifth root of unity, pi=rho−1, R=W[rho], and V=lim_n W[T]_(5)/5^n. There exists a finite unramified extension C/V arising from the generic-special completion of a smooth affine W-curve etale over an open of the T-line, with the following property. Put C'=C⊗_W R and B=C'〈X,Y〉/(X^4+Phi_5(1+pi Y)/pi^4+TXY^3), where angle brackets denote restricted power series and Phi_5(z)=z^4+z^3+z^2+z+1. For g in G=F_5^× set a_g=(rho^g−1)/pi, J_g=(X,Y−a_g)⊂B, and gamma_g=−c_1^dR(J_g). These ideals are invertible. Let E=Q_5(rho), sigma_g(rho)=rho^g, omega_T:G→Z_5^× the Teichmuller character, and e_r=(1/4)sum_g omega_T(g)^(-r)sigma_g for r=1,3. For every b in E with sigma_(-1)b=−b and both e_1b,e_3b nonzero, sum_g sigma_g(b)gamma_g is nonzero in Omega^2_(B/R)[1/5]/d Omega^1_(B/R)[1/5]. The forms are continuous relative to R, retaining dT, and the quotient uses the ordinary differential image.

## proof
1. The special root difference. Over k(T) let H be the projective quartic X^4+Y^4+TXY^3=Z^4, Hcirc its open Z≠0, and P_g=[0:g:1]. A singular point would have Z=0, Y≠0, and s=X/Y satisfying s^4+Ts+1=4s^3+T=0. In characteristic five this implies T=s^3, s^4=2, hence T^4=3, impossible. Thus H is smooth and geometrically integral: distinct projective plane components would meet and produce a singularity.

To show P_1−P_(-1) has infinite order, use the smooth surface M=Spec k[X,U,Y,Y^(-1)]/(XU−(1−Y^4)), mapped to the parameter line by T=(U−X^3)/Y^3. After inverting X the ring is k[X^(±1),Y^(±1)], which is factorial. The four removed divisors L_g=(X,Y−g) have multiplicity one in div(X), and generate Pic(M). Every relation comes from a Laurent unit cX^rY^s, so the only relation is sum_g L_g=0.

The fiber over t in k is w^4=s^4+ts+1 with w invertible. The right side is not a square in k(s): a rational square which is polynomial is a polynomial square, and comparison with (s^2+bs+c)^2 gives b=c=0, contradicting its constant term. Since k contains mu_4, adjoining a fourth root of a nonsquare has degree four. Indeed the extension is a splitting field with Galois group inside mu_4, and a proper subgroup would force the square of the root into k(s). Thus every fiber is integral and reduced. Its unique vertical prime divisor is the principal divisor T−t with multiplicity one. Divisor localization gives Pic(Hcirc∩{Y≠0})=Z^4/Z(1,1,1,1). The points P_g are the images of L_g. Any torsion relation on P_1−P_(-1) in the proper Jacobian would restrict to this lattice and is impossible. This non-torsion property persists after a finite field extension because norm after pullback multiplies divisor classes by its degree.

2. Quotients constructed over an etale parameter cover. Set Delta=256−27T^4. Over W[T,1/Delta] order the roots b_1,...,b_4 of s^4+Ts+1 by a finite etale cover. Write
 a=(b_2−b_4)/(b_2−b_1), x=a(s−b_1)/(s−b_4), lambda=a(b_3−b_1)/(b_3−b_4).
The elements a,a−1,a−lambda,lambda,lambda−1 are units, by the pairwise root differences. Adjoin c with c^4=a(a−1)(a−lambda) and replace w=Z/Y by c w/(s−b_4). Multiplying the transformed factors gives w^4=x(x−1)(x−lambda). Adjoin r with r^2=lambda and alpha_± with alpha_±^2=r±1. These extensions are etale, since the radicands and 2,4 are units.

Put z=w^2/x and v_±=w(x±r)/x. The identities
 z^2=x+r^2/x−1−r^2, v_±^2=z^3+(r±1)^2z
show that z=alpha_±^2 zeta, v_±=alpha_±^3 v gives the constant target E_0:v^2=zeta^3+zeta. The two maps h_+,h_- are quotients by iota(x,w)=(r^2/x,rw/x) and tau iota, respectively, where tau(x,w)=(x,−w). They have degree two because x satisfies a quadratic over the target functions, w is recovered from them and x, and the displayed nontrivial involution fixes those functions. They extend over the proper smooth curves.

Their exact inverse image of {O,(0,0)} is the original Z=0 boundary. The four boundary points are x=0,1,r^2,infinity. At 0 and infinity the orders of (z,v_±) are (−2,−3), so the image is O with local degree one. At 1 and r^2 the orders are (2,1), so the image is (0,0), again with local degree one. Elsewhere z is finite and nonzero, ruling out either target point. Both maps therefore restrict to finite maps of the entire affine Hcirc to E_0circ.

All the equations are over W. Shrinking the parameter open by elements nonzero in the special parameter function field makes them finite flat quotient maps between smooth relative curves. Choose one connected special component of this finite etale parameter cover; it can be isolated in a smooth affine W-model by inverting a lift of its characteristic idempotent. Call the resulting smooth W-algebra D. Its completion at the generic special point is a finite unramified extension C of V, being the corresponding factor of the completed finite etale cover. Further open restrictions become units there.

3. Nonconstant root image and equivariance. The three involutions form a Klein four group. Its total quotient has rational coordinate z=w^2/x: the extension obtained by recovering x and then w has degree at most four, and the four automorphisms fix z, so this is the invariant field. Let q be the quotient by tau. On the Jacobian, the divisor pullback and norm identities give
 q^*q_*+h_+^*h_(+*)+h_-^*h_(-*)=2 id+sum_(g in the Klein group)g=2 id.
The full group norm vanishes since it factors through the Jacobian of the rational total quotient. The class P_1−P_(-1) is killed by q_*. If both other images were torsion, this identity would make it torsion, a contradiction. Choose h with non-torsion image. Since h tau=[−1]h, its image is 2Q, Q=h(P_1). Every point in E_0(k) is defined over a finite field and torsion, so Q is nonconstant. The lifted root section gives Qtilde:Spec D→mathcalE^circ, with this nonconstant reduction.

Let i be the fourth root in W reducing to 2. The rotation [X:Y:Z]↦[iX:iY:Z] fixes s, sends the transformed w to −i w, z to −z, and v_± to −i v_±. It thus induces [−i] on the elliptic target. Since it sends P_1 to P_i, we have h(P_g)=[g^(-1)]Q for all fourth roots g, also on the lifted Teichmuller sections.

4. A bounded trace. For a finite tame double quotient of smooth relative curves there is a trace on total forms relative to W, and then relative to R. Away from ramification it is the etale trace. At ramification, etale-locally an anti-invariant fiber parameter t gives the quotient parameter s=t^2, since 2 is invertible. Invariant fiber one-forms are multiples of t dt=ds/2; invariant base differentials and their wedges descend. Thus invariant total forms are exactly quotient forms. Sum 1+iota^* and descend to obtain a chain map Tr satisfying Tr h^*=2 and h^*Tr=1+iota^*. It is integral, hence continuous, and extends through localization and completion. For any source line L, the Cartier-divisor identity h^*Nm_h L=L⊗iota^*L holds with ramification multiplicities. As h^* is injective on rational cohomology by Tr h^*=2, it follows that Tr(c_1(L))=c_1(Nm_h L). These are identities on ordinary de Rham quotients, since Tr is an actual chain map.

5. Weighted detection on the cyclic model. First take Bcyc=C'〈X,Y〉/(X^4+Y^4+TXY^3−1) and gamma_g^cyc=c_1(O(P_g)), where P_g is its Teichmuller root divisor (X,Y−omega_T(g)). Its norm under h is the graph divisor of [g^(-1)]Qtilde. Choose a proper elliptic unit-root generator eta and dual slope-one generator omega. CM rotation preserves the unit-root line as it commutes with Frobenius. Its character chi on G is one of omega_T and omega_T^3, because [−1]^*=-1 on H^1.

The accepted proof in fact d8c925bb8cff43ee computes the graph-difference Chern class as 2(omega⊗R^*eta−eta⊗R^*omega). The contraction of fact 50b9a29bc7dc842b, with ell(omega)=1 and ell(eta)=0, therefore sends it to 2R^*eta. For R=[g^(-1)]Q this is 2chi(g)^(-1)Q^*eta. The proof of d8c925bb8cff43ee applies scalar fact 868cb19bf6a0a52c to show Q^*eta is nonzero in the ordinary Cohen one-form quotient. Its finite-constant-extension assertion preserves this nonzero class over R.

Write lambda_g=sigma_g(b). Then lambda_(-g)=−lambda_g. Pairing opposite roots and applying Tr followed by the contraction to sum_g lambda_g gamma_g^cyc gives
 (sum_g lambda_g chi(g)^(-1))Q^*eta=4 e_chi(b)Q^*eta,
up to a common polarization sign. The sum over the two opposite elements accounts exactly for the factor two in the graph-difference formula. Since both odd projections of b are nonzero, e_chi(b) is nonzero. A nonzero scalar times the nonzero one-form class remains nonzero. Both operators take exact forms to exact forms, so the weighted source class on Bcyc is nonzero.

6. Formal replacement preserving all root lines. The identity Phi_5(1+pi)=0 gives 5/pi^4≡−1 modulo pi, and every other lower coefficient of Phi_5(1+pi Y)/pi^4 is divisible by pi. Thus B and Bcyc share the special fiber X^4+Y^4+TXY^3=1. Euler's homogeneous identity gives 4G=XG_X+YG_Y for G=X^4+Y^4+TXY^3, so this fiber is smooth. Both models are pi-torsion-free since their equations are monic in Y, and their completions are formally smooth over C'. Lift the identity of their reductions successively modulo pi^n. The resulting continuous C'-linear map is surjective by successive approximation. If an element belongs to its kernel, reduction puts it in pi times the source; target torsion-freeness lets one divide the kernel relation by pi and repeat. Adic separatedness makes the kernel zero.

The ideal J_g is invertible: on its support the Y derivative is the product of the units a_g−a_h for h≠g, so locally the equation expresses Y−a_g as a multiple of X. Its reduction is (X,Y−g), the same as that of the cyclic root ideal. In a complete ring pi belongs to the Jacobson radical; two projective rank-one modules with isomorphic reductions have an isomorphism obtained by lifting the reduced map using projectivity and applying Nakayama at every maximal ideal. Consequently formal replacement carries each Picard class [J_g] to the cyclic root ideal. It fixes C', hence all sigma_g(b), and induces an isomorphism on continuous forms relative to R, retaining dT. The weighted nonvanishing of step 5 therefore holds on B.

## intuition
Tame trace to a constant ordinary elliptic quotient, followed by a bounded contraction, selects one nonzero odd arithmetic character. Formal smoothness transports the root lines to the scaled arithmetic equation.
