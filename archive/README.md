# Original manuscripts

The five files under `original/` reproduce the TeX sources from the supplied
research archive without changes. Their SHA-256 hashes and the original ZIP
hash are recorded in `origin_manifest.json` and checked by `make check`.

These are historical research versions. The consolidated article is
`paper/main.tex`; the supporting audit records explain corrections and the
verification scope. The original PDF files are omitted because they duplicate
the archived sources.
